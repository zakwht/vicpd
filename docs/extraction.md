### Extraction
* Canadian Capital Cities Compiled Dataset include the national capital (Ottawa) and all provincial capital excluding St. John's, NL. St. John's police department is provincially-funded and excluded from this analysis.
* The police operating expenditures budget was extracted as reported on the related financial plan or budget document.
* Collected budget metrics only included operating expenditures.
* The 2024/25 budget was labelled 2025 for cities with budgets that are not aligned to the calendar year; 2025/26 was labelled 2026.
